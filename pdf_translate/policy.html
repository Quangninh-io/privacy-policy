Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

In short:

  • Your PDFs are translated on your device with Apple's built-in translation. The text of your documents is never sent to us or to any translation server, and we do not have a server.
  • Scans, images, signatures and every other PDF tool also work entirely on your device.
  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email or contacts.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads.
  • Subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Niio PDF Translator app for iPhone and iPad (the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Translation

The App translates documents with Apple's Translation framework, which runs on your device. The App reads the text of the pages you choose (using Apple's PDFKit and, for scanned pages, Apple's on-device text recognition), translates it on the device and builds the translated PDF on the device.

To translate between a pair of languages for the first time, iOS may ask you to download a language pack. That download is performed by iOS from Apple and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy; it does not include your documents. You can manage language packs in the App's Settings or in iOS Settings.

2. Your documents and files

Everything else you do with your documents — viewing, comparing, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, editing, signing, adding watermarks, page numbers or passwords, and scanning — also happens locally on your device. The files you open, import, scan, translate or create are stored in the App's own storage on your device.

We do not upload, read, copy, store or share the contents of your documents. A file leaves your device only when you choose to send it somewhere yourself, for example with "Share" or "Save to Files".

Passwords you enter to open or protect a PDF are used only on your device to perform that operation. They are not saved or transmitted.

3. Permissions the App uses

Camera

Used only when you scan a document. Scanning uses Apple's VisionKit document scanner, which detects page edges on the device. We do not record or keep camera images other than the pages you save.

Photos

Used only when you pick images to turn into a PDF, add an image or an imported signature to a page, or choose to save converted pages to your photo library. The App only accesses the photos you select.

Files

Used when you import a PDF with the system file picker, open a PDF in the App from another app, or save a copy to a location you choose.

4. Information stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of information locally so it works the way you left it: your settings (such as appearance, default target language and quality preferences), your list of translations and documents, cached thumbnails, and whether you have an active Premium subscription. This information stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App. You can also clear the cache from the App's Settings.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you use the free version, the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit the frequency of and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers (such as the identifier for vendors and, only if you allow tracking, Apple's advertising identifier);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (views and taps) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings › Ad privacy options. You can also limit tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by the Apple App Store. Apple handles your payment information under its own privacy policy; we never receive your card details or billing address. The App asks the App Store, on your device, whether your Apple ID has an active subscription so it can unlock Premium features.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. The only third parties involved in the App are Google (advertising) and Apple (purchases, on-device translation and language packs), as described above.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help address it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because your documents, translations and settings are stored only on your device, you control them completely. You can delete individual files in the App, clear the cache in Settings, or remove everything by uninstalling the App. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

10. Security

Your files remain in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security. PDFs you protect with a password are encrypted. No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep your device and passwords safe.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to certain processing. Since we do not collect personal information ourselves, most requests concern data handled by Google for advertising — you can exercise those through the settings above or through Google. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with its effective date.

13. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, contact us at ninhdevelop.io@gmail.com.
