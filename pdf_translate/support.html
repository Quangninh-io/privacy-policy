Frequently asked questions

How do I translate a PDF?

Open the Translate tab, pick a PDF from Files or from My Documents, choose the target language and the output style — Same layout (translation placed where the original text was) or Bilingual (original page followed by its translation) — then start. The finished file appears in your translations and in Documents.

Is my document uploaded anywhere to be translated?

No. Translation uses Apple's on-device translation, so the text of your documents never leaves your iPhone or iPad. That also means translation works offline once the language pack is installed.

It asks me to download a language

The first time you translate between two languages, iOS needs that language pack. Tap Download when asked and keep the app open until it finishes (an internet connection is needed only for this download). You can manage packs in Settings › Language packs.

Some pages came out untranslated or blank

Pages that are pictures (scans or photos) are read with on-device text recognition; very blurry, handwritten or tiny text may not be recognised. Try a clearer scan, or use Compare with original on the file to check each page side by side.

How many pages can I translate for free?

Free translations cover the first 10 pages of each document. Premium translates documents of any length.

Where are my files stored?

Files you open, scan, translate or create are kept in the app's own storage on your device and listed under Documents. Nothing is uploaded to any server. You can also see them in the Files app under On My iPhone › PDF Translate. To keep a copy elsewhere, use Share or Save to Files.

The scanner won't open the camera

The app needs camera permission to scan. Go to iOS Settings › PDF Translate › Camera and turn it on.

I forgot the password of a PDF I locked

Passwords are never stored or sent anywhere, so neither we nor anyone else can recover them. If you still have the original unlocked file, use that one.

What does Premium include?

Premium translates documents of any length, removes all ads and unlocks every tool: signatures, organizing pages, watermarks, page numbers, locking and unlocking PDFs, and PDF repair. Free users can use a premium tool once by watching a short ad.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then open the app and tap Settings › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app: iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Niio PDF Translator › Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or the free trial) to avoid being charged for the next one. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How can I change my ad privacy choices?

If you were asked for ad consent, you can change your choice anytime in Settings › Ad privacy options. You can also limit tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Premium subscribers see no ads at all.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device. Delete files in Documents, clear the cache in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
