01Overview

Niio is a pregnancy-tracking app: due date and current week, daily baby-development notes, a kick counter and contraction timer, symptom and weight logs, an appointment calendar, a hospital-bag checklist, and baby-name ideas. Niio doesn't run its own servers and doesn't need an account to work — the only network activity comes from the ad and payment services described below. This page explains what that means for your data.

02Information We Don't Collect

Niio doesn't ask for an account, an email address, or a phone number — there's no sign-up screen, because there's nothing to sign up for. We don't run analytics or crash-reporting SDKs inside the app, so we don't collect usage statistics, location, contacts, photos, or browsing history.

In short: if a data point isn't listed elsewhere on this page, Niio doesn't collect it.

03Pregnancy Details You Enter

During onboarding you enter your first name and your due date (or the date of your last period or conception, which Niio converts to an estimated due date). Niio uses this to calculate your current week of pregnancy and to show week-by-week content. As you use the app you can also log symptoms, weight, kick counts, contraction timings, appointments, and a hospital-bag checklist — all of that is for your own reference and is never transmitted anywhere by us.

04Local Storage & Retention

Your name, due date, onboarding status, and Premium subscription status are saved in your device's standard app storage — no separate database, no cloud sync — so Niio remembers them the next time you open the app. Everything else you log — symptoms, weight entries, kick-counter and contraction sessions, hospital-bag checklist state, and favorite baby names — lives only in memory for your current session and clears the next time you fully close and reopen the app. Uninstalling Niio, or clearing its storage from your device's settings, permanently deletes everything it has saved.

05Advertising (Google AdMob)

Niio shows ads through Google AdMob to support the free version of the app. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical information (device type, general region, app activity) to select and measure ads, and, where you've allowed it, to personalize them. We don't control what Google does with that information beyond serving ads in the app — Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and, on iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt govern that collection. Upgrading to Premium removes ads, and this data collection along with them.

06In-App Purchases

Niio Premium is a subscription — monthly or annual, with a free trial on eligible accounts — billed and processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, using the payment method already on file with your Apple ID or Google account. Niio never sees or stores your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail — we only receive the store's confirmation of an active subscription, which we use to unlock Premium on your device.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; you can review the exact price, trial terms, or cancel at any time from your App Store or Play Store account settings. Tapping Restore Purchases works the same way in reverse: Niio asks the store to confirm your entitlement and unlocks accordingly.

07Third-Party Services

Google AdMob and Apple's / Google's own store infrastructure — covered above — are the only third parties Niio relies on. We don't embed any other third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising software. Links out to support or legal pages open in your device's browser rather than inside the app; those pages, and the app stores themselves, have their own privacy practices, which this policy doesn't cover.

08Children's Privacy

Niio is a pregnancy-tracking app and is not directed at, or intended for use by, children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

09Your Rights & Choices

Because almost everything Niio stores lives on your device, you're already in control of it: uninstall the app, or clear its storage, and your name, due date, and Premium status are gone immediately — there's no account to delete and no server-side copy to request, correct, or export, because none exists. To limit ad personalization, use your device's ad-tracking settings (App Tracking Transparency on iOS, or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android) — Niio will still show ads, just less targeted ones. For anything tied to a purchase — receipts, refunds, or subscription history — those records are held by Apple or Google, and their standard account tools apply.

10Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Niio after a change means you accept the update. We won't use a change to this policy to start collecting data it didn't previously disclose without telling you first.
