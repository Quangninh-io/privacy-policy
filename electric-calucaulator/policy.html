Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Electric Calculator mobile app for Android and iOS (the “App”) handles information. The App is published by its independent developer (“we”, “us”).

In short: we do not collect, store or sell your personal information. The App has no accounts and no servers. What you type into it stays on your device. The App is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob may collect some device information as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App keeps the following only on your device, in the app’s local storage:

  • Your settings (theme, decimal precision, default voltage and frequency, units)
  • Your favorite calculators and recently used calculators
  • Your calculation history (the values you entered and the results)

We never receive this information. You can clear your calculation history at any time in History → Clear All. Uninstalling the App removes all of it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), provided by Google LLC. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, or the iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This collection is performed by Google under Google’s own policies. We do not receive your personal information from Google; we only see aggregated reports about ad performance.

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy Choices.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission before accessing your IDFA. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path may vary by device).

If you decline personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.

3. Features that leave the App only when you use them

  • Share: when you tap Share, your device’s share sheet sends the text you chose to the app you pick.
  • Contact Support: opens your email app. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to reply to you.
  • Rate the App / links: opens the App Store, Google Play or a web page in the corresponding app or browser.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience tool for electrical calculations and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data security and retention

Data stored by the App stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on a server. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google’s policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold your personal data, most of these requests concern Google as the advertising provider. You can manage ad personalization through the choices in section 2, and you may contact us with any question.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at ninhdevelop.io@gmail.com.
