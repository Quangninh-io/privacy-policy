Frequently asked questions

How do I save a favorite calculator?

Open any calculator and tap the ⭐ icon. Your favorites appear in My Tools, together with your recently used calculators.

How do I save, share or export a result as PDF?

After calculating, use the buttons under the result. Save keeps it in your history, Copy copies it, Share sends it to another app, and PDF creates a PDF with your inputs, the result and the formula used.

How do I delete my calculation history?

Go to History. Use ⋮ → Delete on an entry to remove it, or Clear All to remove everything. History is stored only on your device.

How do I switch between metric and imperial, or mm² and AWG?

Open Settings → Units. Choose the Unit System (metric or imperial) and the Wire Size standard: mm² uses IEC tables and AWG uses NEC tables.

Can I change the default voltage, frequency, currency or decimal places?

Yes. In Settings → General you can set the Default Voltage (pre-filled in single-phase calculators), Default Frequency (50 or 60 Hz, used for reactance, motors and capacitors), Currency (used by the electricity cost calculator) and Decimal Precision.

How do I change the language or turn on dark mode?

Open Settings → General and tap Language or Dark Mode. The app is available in English, Vietnamese, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Thai and Russian.

A result looks wrong. What should I do?

First check the units next to each field (for example W vs kW, or m vs ft) and the settings above, such as the voltage, frequency and wire size standard. Each calculator shows the formula it used under the result.

If the result still looks wrong, email us the calculator name, the values you entered and the result you expected, and we will look into it.

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Premium → Remove Ads and subscribe to the monthly plan. It removes all banner and full-screen ads. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it.

I paid, but I still see ads. How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy, then tap Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases. If ads still appear, email us and include your order ID from the purchase receipt.

How do I cancel my subscription or request a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. Tap Settings → Premium → Manage Subscription, or:

  • iPhone / iPad: iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds: Google Play refund help

Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy → Privacy Choices to change your consent for personalized ads. On iPhone you can also control tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Does the app work offline? Do I need an account?

All calculations run on your device, so they work without an internet connection. There is no account or sign-up. An internet connection is only needed for ads and in-app purchases.

Safety disclaimer

Electric Calculator is a reference tool. Results are estimates based on standard formulas and tables and may not account for every site condition. Always follow your local electrical codes, and have a qualified electrician design, check and carry out work on electrical installations.
