Frequently asked questions

How do I open a PDF?

Tap Open PDF on the Home screen and choose a file. You can also open a PDF from another app — in Files, Mail, Messages or your file manager, tap the PDF, then Share › Niio PDF (iPhone/iPad) or Open with › Niio PDF (Android).

Where are my files stored?

Files you open, scan or create are kept in the app's own storage on your device and listed under My Files. Nothing is uploaded to any server. On iPhone and iPad you can also see them in the Files app under On My iPhone › Niio PDF. To keep a copy elsewhere, open the file and use Share or Save to device.

How do I edit, sign or work on a PDF?

Open the PDF and use the toolbar at the bottom: Edit (text, images, drawing, highlights, shapes), Sign, Pages (reorder, rotate, delete, extract), Compress, To Image and More for every other tool. Edited files are saved as a new copy, so your original is never overwritten.

The scanner won't open the camera

The app needs camera permission to scan. On iPhone/iPad go to Settings › Niio PDF › Camera and turn it on. On Android go to Settings › Apps › Niio PDF › Permissions › Camera.

I forgot the password of a PDF I locked

Passwords are applied with strong AES-256 encryption and are never stored or sent anywhere, so neither we nor anyone else can recover them. If you still have the original unlocked file, use that one.

A PDF won't open

If the file is password-protected, the app will ask for its password. If it says the file can't be opened, it may be damaged — try Repair PDF from the Home screen. If that doesn't help, email us and tell us where the file came from.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks PDF signatures, watermarks, the page organizer, extracting and removing pages, password protection and unlocking, PDF to Text, page numbers and PDF repair — and removes all ads. Free users can try a premium tool once by watching a short ad.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe, then open the app and tap Settings › Restore Purchases (or Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Premium screen).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside the app:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Niio PDF Tools › Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Niio PDF Tools › Cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or the free trial) to avoid being charged for the next one. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple and Google, so refunds are handled by them: on Apple devices request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android use the Google Play refund process at Google Play Help.

How can I change my ad privacy choices?

If you were asked for ad consent, you can change your choice anytime in Settings › Ad Privacy Options. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings. Premium subscribers see no ads at all.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device. Delete files in My Files, clear the cache and history in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
