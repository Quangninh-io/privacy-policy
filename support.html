Support

Support for apps by this developer

Need help with the app, have feedback, or found a bug? We're happy to help.

Email us at ninhdevelop.io@gmail.com — we typically reply within 1–2 business days. Please mention the app name so we can help faster.

How do I remove ads?

Subscribe to Premium. Ads are removed immediately after subscribing. From the app, open Settings and tap the Premium / Upgrade option to see plans and pricing.

Is there a free trial?

Some plans include a free trial for new subscribers. If a plan has one, the length is shown before you subscribe, and you won't be charged until the trial ends — cancel anytime before it ends to avoid being charged.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple or Google, not by us — we can't cancel it on your behalf. To cancel:

  • iPhone/iPad: Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → select the app → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Open the Play Store app → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → select the app → Cancel

You'll keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.

I resubscribed / got a new phone — how do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, tap the Premium / Upgrade option, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe originally.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Data you enter into the app is processed and stored only on your device — nothing is uploaded to us or any server unless the app explicitly says otherwise. See the app's Privacy Policy (linked in the app's Settings screen or its store listing) for full details.

I found a bug or have a feature request

Email us at ninhdevelop.io@gmail.com with the app name and as much detail as possible (what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and your device model/OS version if relevant). We read every message.
