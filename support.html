Support

App: Niio: Finance Calculator

Need help with the app, have feedback, or found a bug? We're happy to help.

Email us at ninhdevelop.io@gmail.com — we typically reply within 1–2 business days.

How do I remove ads?

Subscribe to Niio Premium (Monthly or Annual). Ads are removed immediately after subscribing. From the app, go to the Profile tab and tap "Go Premium" to see plans and pricing.

Is there a free trial?

New subscribers are eligible for a 3-day free trial on the Annual plan. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before it ends to avoid being charged. The Monthly plan does not include a free trial.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple, not by us — we can't cancel it on your behalf. To cancel:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad
  • Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions
  • Select Niio: Finance Calculator and tap Cancel Subscription

You'll keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.

I resubscribed / got a new phone — how do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to the Profile tab, tap "Go Premium", then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe originally.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Every number you enter — income, mortgage amount, retirement savings — is calculated and stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us or any server. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I found a bug or have a feature request

Email us at ninhdevelop.io@gmail.com with as much detail as possible (what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and your device model/iOS version if relevant). We read every message.
