01Overview

BeatFlash pulses your device's torch in sync with a song's beat — nothing more. It doesn't need an internet connection to do that, and it doesn't have a server sitting behind it collecting anything while you use it. The short version of this whole policy: BeatFlash works almost entirely on your device, so almost nothing about you ever has to leave it.

02Information We Don't Collect

BeatFlash doesn't ask for an account, an email address, or a name — there's no sign-up screen, because there's nothing to sign up for. We don't run analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs inside the app, so we don't collect usage statistics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or browsing history either.

In short: if a data point isn't listed elsewhere on this page, BeatFlash doesn't collect it.

03Camera & Flashlight Access

BeatFlash requests access to your device's flash hardware so it can switch the torch on and off in time with the music — the same physical LED your camera app uses. BeatFlash never activates the camera sensor, and never takes, stores, or transmits photos or video. If another app is already using the torch (an active camera session, for example), BeatFlash simply can't turn it on until that app releases it; we don't request any broader camera permission than the light itself.

04Your Music Files

When you add a song from your device's storage, BeatFlash reads that file locally to play it back and to generate its flash pattern. Both the file and the pattern stay on your device — BeatFlash has no upload feature and no cloud library, so your music is never sent anywhere, by us or to us.

05In-App Purchases

BeatFlash Premium is a one-time, $9.99 unlock — pay once, keep it forever, no subscription and no recurring charge. Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, using the payment method already on file with your Apple ID or Google account. BeatFlash never sees or stores your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail — we only receive the store's confirmation that a purchase went through, which we use to unlock Premium on your device.

Tapping Restore Purchase works the same way in reverse: BeatFlash asks the store to confirm your entitlement and unlocks accordingly. For anything about the payment itself — receipts, refunds, disputed charges — Apple's and Google's own privacy and billing policies apply, not ours.

06Local Storage & Retention

The only information BeatFlash keeps is stored in standard app preferences, on your device: whether you've unlocked Premium, how many free sessions you've used, and your flash-intensity setting. None of it is backed up to a BeatFlash server, because there isn't one to back it up to. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device's settings, permanently deletes all of it.

07Third-Party Services

BeatFlash links out to a small number of pages — support, terms, and this policy — which open in your device's browser rather than inside the app. Those pages, and the app stores themselves, have their own privacy practices; this policy only covers BeatFlash the app itself. Beyond that, BeatFlash doesn't embed any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

08Children's Privacy

BeatFlash is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age — there's no mechanism in the app to collect it from any user in the first place.

09Your Rights & Choices

Because your data lives on your device, you're already in control of it: uninstall BeatFlash, or clear its storage, and everything it has stored locally is gone immediately. There's no account to delete and no server-side copy to request, correct, or export, because none exists. For anything tied to a purchase — receipts, refunds, or payment history — those records are held by Apple or Google, and their standard account tools apply.

10Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use BeatFlash after a change means you accept the update. We won't use a change to this policy to start collecting data it didn't previously disclose without telling you first.
