Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the results?

BuildCalc uses standard engineering formulas and common rules of thumb: a 1.54 dry-volume factor for concrete, 1.33 for mortar, and typical wastage and steel percentages. Each calculator explains its method in the 💡 note before you calculate. Results are estimates for planning and ordering materials. Always check structural work against your drawings, local codes and a qualified engineer.

Which calculators are free?

Concrete, Area, Brick, Paint, Wall and the Knowledge Center are free. Premium unlocks all 50+ calculators and tools (unit converter, scientific calculator, Bill of Quantities), removes ads, lets you set your own local material prices and keeps unlimited history.

How do I change units or currency?

Open Settings and choose Metric or Imperial, and your currency. Each calculator also has a unit switcher (ft, in, m, cm) at the top. Small dimensions such as brick sizes are entered in millimetres (metric) or inches (imperial).

How do I subscribe, and what does it cost?

BuildCalc Premium is a single monthly subscription. Tap any locked calculator or Settings → BuildCalc Premium. The price is shown in your local currency before you confirm, and the subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  • Android: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → BuildCalc → Cancel. Or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BuildCalc → Cancel Subscription. Or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

You keep Premium until the end of the period you have paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

I paid but Premium is not unlocked / I changed phones

Make sure you are signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used to subscribe, then open Settings → Restore Purchases (or tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen). If it still does not unlock, email us with your order number from the store receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store that processed the payment. On Google Play, see Google Play refund help. On the App Store, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my saved calculations? Can I back them up?

Saved calculations are kept only on your device, under the History tab. They are removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Use Share on a result to send a copy to email or notes if you want to keep it elsewhere.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep the free calculators free. A full-screen ad appears at most once every few calculations. Premium removes all ads. See our Privacy Policy for how ads use data.
