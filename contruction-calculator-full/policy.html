Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BuildCalc – Construction Calculator (the “App”), published by Ninhkeyio (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App on Android or iOS.

In short: BuildCalc has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your calculations, project names and settings stay on your device. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally, in the App’s private storage on your device:

  • Saved calculations: project names, inputs and results in your History.
  • Preferences: unit system, currency, concrete mix standard and your material prices.
  • Whether you have completed onboarding and whether your Premium subscription is active.

This information is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time with History → Clear All, or by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows banner, native and full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. Premium subscribers do not see ads. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID or, where permitted, Apple’s IDFA.
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version).
  • Ad interactions (impressions and clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash information from the ads SDK.

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • iOS: the App does not ask for permission to track you, so your IDFA is not shared with advertisers through the App. You can also manage this under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Upgrading to Premium removes all ads.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

BuildCalc Premium is a monthly auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store; we never receive your card or billing details. The App asks the store, on your device, whether your subscription is active so it can unlock Premium features. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing results

When you tap Share, the App passes a text summary of your calculation to the app you choose (for example email or a messaging app). We do not receive a copy.

6. Links to other services

The App may open web pages, your email app or the app store. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Children

The App is a tool for builders and DIY users and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and security

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google for advertising or purchases is retained according to Google’s and Apple’s policies. We do not operate servers that store your data.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are best handled through the device controls above or with Google or Apple directly. You can always contact us for help.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
